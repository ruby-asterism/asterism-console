# S3: a board drives the rover, the console watches

Part of Asterism stage S3 (family-mruby: a Family mruby board drives the
S1 ROS 2 rover with its arrow keys and shows the rover's camera and
odometry on its own screen; the report is
fmruby-core/doc/ruby_asterism/report/s3.md, the step-by-step demo is in
family-mruby's `docker/mujoco/README.md`). Checked 2026-10-10 against
zenohd 1.10.1, ROS 2 Jazzy (rmw_zenoh) with mujoco_ros2_control and a
P4 board on WiFi. **No change to the console was needed.**

## What the console shows during the drive

- **Graph**: the rover's ROS 2 nodes and the board's node
  (`/fmruby_cam_<board>`), which publishes `/cmd_vel` (to
  `/twist_stamper`) and subscribes `/odom` (from `/diff_drive_controller`)
  and `/camera/image/compressed` (from `/camera_jpeg`).
- **Watch**: `0/camera/image/compressed/**` shows the camera following the
  drive.
- **Plots** (a second tab): `/odom` `pose.pose.position.x` and `.y`.
- **Recording**: `/cmd_vel`, `/odom`, `/ground_truth/odom`, the camera and
  the network structure, from the Recordings form.
- **Timeline**: `/odom` x and orientation.z plotted, the camera frame and
  "At the cursor" (odometry, true pose, the command) following the cursor;
  "Network at the cursor" rewinds the graph with the board's node in it.

## The live check (2026-10-10)

| Item | Result |
|---|---|
| Graph | 13 ROS 2 nodes, 29 topics; the board's node with its three edges |
| Watch | the camera picture changing with the drive (the red pillar ahead, then the green pillar and the purple box after the turn) |
| Plots | `/odom` x from 0 to 1.15 m during the drive: 429 messages, all plotted |
| Recording | 41 s: 5,204 messages, 4.4 MB, 205 camera frames (5.0 Hz), 75 `/cmd_vel` (the number the board sent), lost 0 |
| Timeline | at 8.0 s (driving forward, x about 0.5) the red pillar ahead and `/cmd_vel` linear 0.2; at 20.0 s (after the turn) the green pillar and the box, `/cmd_vel` 0 |
| Rewind | "Network at the cursor" steps through the five changes of the recording with the board's node present |

The whole demo was also run from the README top to bottom (a second
recording of 41 s: 5,206 messages, 205 camera frames, lost 0), with the
recording started from the Recordings form.

## Notes

- One driver at a time: the board sends `/cmd_vel` only while it moves
  the rover and one stop when it stops, so a playback from the console
  ("Play" on the network) should not run while the board drives.
- The camera stream still needs `camera_watch.py` on the rover's side
  for the console alone (S1); while the board runs, its subscription keeps
  `republish` going as well.
