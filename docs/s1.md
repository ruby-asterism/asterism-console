# S1: camera pictures (sensor_msgs/CompressedImage)

Part of Asterism stage S1 (family-mruby: a MuJoCo two-wheeled robot driven
over ROS 2; its report is fmruby-core/doc/ruby_asterism/report/s1.md). Done
2026-10-09 and checked against the robot: zenohd 1.10.1, ROS 2 Jazzy
(rmw_zenoh) with mujoco_ros2_control, a 160x120 JPEG camera at 5 Hz.

## What it adds

A `sensor_msgs/msg/CompressedImage` whose data is a JPEG or PNG is shown
as a picture:

- **the watch panel**: the latest picture above the list of values;
- **the details of a topic** (the rates' last message): the picture, then
  the one-line preview (stamp, frame_id, format, bytes);
- **the timeline** of a recording: the selected channel's message at the
  cursor as a picture (its `data` field in the decoded value is replaced by
  "(N bytes, image/jpeg)"), and a thumbnail in "At the cursor".

`Bridge::Payload.image(bytes, type)` reads the CDR (header, format, the
data's length) and gives `{ mime, data (base64), bytes, width, height }`;
the kind is taken from the data's magic number (FF D8 or the PNG
signature), not from the `format` string ("rgb8; jpeg compressed bgr8").
Width and height come from the JPEG start-of-frame or the PNG IHDR. The
page puts it in an `<img src="data:...">`, scaled to 320 px wide without
smoothing (`image-rendering: pixelated`).

## Bounds and costs

- At most `Payload::MAX_IMAGE` (256 KB) of picture per message; larger ones
  have no picture (the preview line still shows).
- The rates keep the whole last message of an image topic (up to 256 KB +
  4 KB) instead of the first 4 KB, so the details can show it. One such
  buffer per image topic measured.
- What reaches the page: the rates' row of a changed topic once a second
  (one picture a second per measured image topic, about 3.7 KB of base64 for
  a 2.8 KB frame), the watch panel up to 10 values a second per key.

## Found on the way

- **Lazy publishers stay silent for the console.** image_transport's
  `republish` (the usual way to make the JPEG topic) compresses only while
  a ROS subscriber is matched; rmw_zenoh counts subscribers by their
  liveliness tokens. The bridge's subscriptions (rates, watch, plots,
  recordings) are plain Zenoh subscribers without a token, so a camera
  behind `republish` sends nothing while only the console looks. The
  family-mruby rover keeps a small ROS subscriber (`camera_watch.py`) on the
  topic. A console-side fix would be to announce a subscriber token (as
  the playback announces its publishers) while recording or watching a
  ROS 2 topic; not done.
